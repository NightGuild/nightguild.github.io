Economy & progression

Progression systems reward activity and give members goals to work toward.

Currency

Use the economy commands available in your server to view your balance and learn which activities earn or spend currency. Reward values and cooldowns can change.

Levels and upgrades

Read upgrade details before spending currency, especially when an upgrade affects a guild or another player.
