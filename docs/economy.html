FEATURE GUIDE

Economy

The economy gives you a balance to build up and spend on parts of the bot. Guilds and other activities can also tie into longer-term progression.

Checking your balance

In a server where NightGuildBot is installed, type / and look for the economy or balance command. Choose it from Discord's list and follow the options shown there. I’m keeping exact command names out of this page until the live list is final, so you don't end up trying commands that have changed.

Earning currency

Different bot activities can award currency. The amount and any cooldown depend on the activity, so check the response you get after using a feature rather than relying on an old screenshot or guide.

Spending and upgrades

Before you confirm a purchase or upgrade, check the price and what it actually changes. If the action has a confirmation step, take a moment to read it. For anything that affects a guild, make sure you're happy with the change on behalf of the group.

Shared economy

NightGuildBot is being built around one main currency and connected progression systems. Some guild-related features may use shared progress, while your personal balance and collection are still tied to your own Discord account.

If your balance looks wrong

  • Check the bot's most recent response, not an older message.
  • Make sure you're looking at the right server and account.
  • Don't repeat a purchase because the first response was slow. Check whether it went through first.
  • Tell the server administrator what happened and include the command's error message.
Prices, rewards and progression rules are still subject to change. The bot's current response is the source to trust.
