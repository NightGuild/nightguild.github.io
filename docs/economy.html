Economy & progression

The economy and progression systems are intended to give players rewards, upgrades, and longer-term goals.

Balances and currency

Use the economy commands available in your server to view your balance and learn which activities earn or spend currency. Always read the amount and confirmation details before making a purchase or transfer.

Earning rewards

Different activities may award currency or progression. Reward amounts, cooldowns, and eligibility rules can change as the bot is developed. Check the response from the relevant command for the current rules.

Levels and upgrades

Progression may include levels, upgrade paths, and unlocks. Read an upgrade's description and cost before confirming it, especially if it affects a shared guild feature.

Good habits

  • Check your balance before spending.
  • Read upgrade descriptions carefully.
  • Do not assume rewards or cooldowns are identical across versions.
  • Ask staff if a transaction result looks incorrect.
Exact economy command names, prices, rewards, and progression requirements should be taken from the current bot responses, not guessed from this overview.
