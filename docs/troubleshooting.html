Troubleshooting

A few checks can help narrow down common NightGuildBot issues.

A command does not appear

Check that the command is registered and deployed, and that your permissions allow you to use it. Restarting the bot alone may not register new slash commands.

Music does not play

Check voice permissions, try another URL, confirm FFmpeg and audio libraries are available, and review console logs for resolver or voice errors.
