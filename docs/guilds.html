Guilds

Guilds are the group side of NightGuildBot: a place for people to team up, manage their members and work towards shared goals.

Private botYou’ll need access to a server where NightGuildBot is already installed.

Finding the guild commands

  1. Open a text channel in a server where the bot is online.
  2. Type / and select NightGuildBot from Discord's command picker.
  3. Look for the guild-related commands. Select one to see the options Discord asks you to fill in.
  4. Read the bot's reply before confirming an action. Some actions may be followed by buttons on a guild post.

Guild posts and management

A guild can have a dedicated post with its details and management controls. If you're the owner or have the right permissions, the buttons on that post may let you manage parts of the guild without having to remember another command.

Members and shared progress

Guilds are meant to be a team feature. Check the guild post for the current member list and any shared progress information. Some management actions are reserved for the guild owner or people with the appropriate permissions.

A couple of things to keep in mind

  • Check whether your group already has a guild before creating another one.
  • Read the confirmation message carefully before making changes to a guild.
  • If a guild post or button seems out of date, don't repeat the action several times. Ask the server's bot administrator to check it.
The exact command names, limits and permissions can change while the bot is being developed. The live Discord command picker and the bot's own replies show what's currently enabled.
