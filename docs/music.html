FEATURE GUIDE

Music

The music feature is designed for listening together in a Discord voice channel.

Before playing

  • Join the voice channel where you want the bot to play.
  • Make sure NightGuildBot has permission to view and connect to that channel.
  • Check that the bot is online and that music is enabled in the current deployment.

Starting playback

Open the slash-command picker and find the music playback command available in your server. Choose it, enter a supported search query or URL if requested, and submit. The bot should respond with the playback result or an explanation if it cannot load the track.

Queue controls

Queue, skip, pause, resume, and stop controls depend on which commands are enabled in the current build. Use the command picker to see the exact controls available to you.

Supported sources

Source support depends on the installed audio resolver and current provider availability. A link may be used to identify a track and find a playable match rather than stream directly from that service.

If playback fails, check voice-channel permissions and see Troubleshooting. Exact music command names and supported providers may change.
