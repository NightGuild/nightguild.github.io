FEATURE GUIDE

Fishing

Fishing is a collection-focused feature where players can catch fish and work toward discovering more species.

Getting started

Open Discord's slash-command picker and look for the fishing-related commands available to you. Select a command to see the options, then follow the instructions in the bot's response.

Rods and equipment

If equipment or rod upgrades are enabled in your current version, review their descriptions and costs before equipping or purchasing them. Different equipment may affect the fishing experience.

Your catches and Fishdex

Use the collection or Fishdex feature, if enabled, to review discovered fish and your progress. Rarity labels and catch counts help you track what you have found.

Tips

  • Read the result of each fishing action; it may include useful catch details.
  • Check your collection to see which species you have not discovered.
  • Review equipment information before spending currency.
Fishing commands, rod statistics, catch rates, and available equipment can change during development. The live bot is the source of truth for exact values.
