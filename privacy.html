Privacy policy

What NightGuildBot may need to remember to make its features work.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Please review before publishing. This page describes a typical setup based on the project's features. Confirm it against the bot's actual database, logs, hosting provider and music services before treating it as final.

Information the bot may process

Depending on which features are enabled, NightGuildBot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames or display names, guild membership and progression, currency balances, inventory and fishing records, command usage, and settings needed to run the bot. Discord may also provide message or interaction content when a command needs it.

Why it is used

This information is used to respond to commands, keep game progress and settings, manage guild features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and keep the bot running. The bot should not need your Discord password.

Where it is stored

Data may be stored in the bot's database and in operational logs on the machine or hosting service where the bot runs. The exact storage location, backups, and retention period depend on the current deployment. Do not publish this page until those details have been checked.

Sharing and service providers

Data may pass through Discord when you use Discord features. If music or other integrations are enabled, the relevant provider may receive the information needed to make that feature work. Hosting providers may also process technical data. This page should be updated to name any additional providers the bot actually uses.

Retention and deletion

Some records need to remain stored so features such as balances, guilds and collections continue to work. Records may also remain in backups or logs for a period of time. A user-data deletion process and a clear retention period should be set up and documented before this policy is considered complete.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot or remove it from a server. For a question or a request about data associated with your Discord account, contact the developer through the official support server. Add the real support invite here and make sure requests can actually be handled.

Children and privacy

Don't use the bot in a way that violates Discord's rules or the laws that apply to you. If you believe personal information has been handled improperly, contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when features, hosting, or data storage change. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact the NightGuildBot developer through the official support server. Replace this instruction with a working contact method before launch.
