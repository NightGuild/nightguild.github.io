Terms of use

A few ground rules for using NightGuildBot and this website.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Using the bot

By using NightGuildBot, you agree to use it lawfully and follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Don't use the bot to harass people, spam, disrupt servers, exploit bugs, or interfere with the service.

Features and availability

NightGuildBot is an independent project that is still being worked on. Features can change, break, be reset, or be removed, and downtime can happen. I can't promise that the bot or any particular feature will always be available or that saved progress will never be lost.

Server owners and moderators

Server staff are responsible for how they configure and use the bot in their server. Please make sure the bot's permissions make sense for your community. Don't grant permissions that it doesn't need.

Abuse and access

Access may be limited if someone abuses the bot, attacks its infrastructure, or repeatedly disrupts its use. Where practical, issues and mistakes should be reported rather than exploited.

Third-party services

The bot relies on Discord and may use other services for features such as music. Those services have their own terms and policies. NightGuildBot is not operated by or affiliated with Discord unless explicitly stated.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the project changes. The latest version will be posted on this page. If you don't agree with a change, stop using the bot.

Contact

For questions about these terms, contact the bot's developer through the official NightGuildBot support server. Add your real support invite here before publishing this page.
